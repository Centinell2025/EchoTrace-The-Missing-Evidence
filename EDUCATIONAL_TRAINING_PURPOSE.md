# EDUCATIONAL AND FORENSIC TRAINING USE NOTICE

## EchoTrace — The Missing Evidence

**Developed by Beacon of the Eagle LLC — Centinell Suite Defense**

### Purpose
EchoTrace is a fictional, controlled learning environment created **exclusively for education, professional training, and technical skill development** in Digital Forensic Technology, Digital Forensics and Incident Response (DFIR), Forensic Engineering, evidence preservation, system recovery, and enterprise resilience.

Its scenarios, investigation questions, laboratories, databases, training records, and instructor materials are designed to help learners practice lawful, evidence-based forensic methodologies. They are **not reports of real incidents** and do not represent actual compromises of any named organization or person.

### Synthetic and simulated materials
All organizations, identities, hostnames, network addresses, user accounts, system events, SQL records, backup statuses, outages, and suspected malicious activities in EchoTrace are fictional or synthetically generated for learning purposes. Any reference to AI-assisted attack activity is an investigative hypothesis in a training exercise, **not a verified attribution**.

The project does not intentionally contain live credentials, personal customer records, actual confidential business datasets, executable ransomware, destructive malware, or instructions to compromise systems without permission. Exercises must be conducted with supplied training evidence or on systems the trainee is explicitly authorized to use.

### Forensic and professional standards
Learners are expected to:
- acquire and preserve evidence lawfully;
- maintain evidence provenance, integrity checks, and chain-of-custody records;
- reconstruct timelines using corroborated sources;
- distinguish facts, hypotheses, interpretations, and uncertainty;
- validate recoverability and backups in isolated environments;
- document methods, limitations, confidence, and findings;
- prioritize safe containment, recovery, and business continuity.

Evidence, findings, conclusions, and outcomes produced in training are **simulated** and must not be presented as independently verified operational or security incidents.

### Training scope and development status
EchoTrace is structured around 25 specialist laboratory modules and a catalogue of 100 forensic scenarios. Scenario outlines and provisional instructor notes do not by themselves establish that every lab has been fully implemented or independently validated. Only exercises with published evidence and completed testing should be described as execution-ready.

### Intended audience
Cybersecurity students; SOC and DFIR analysts; forensic technicians; database, storage, and infrastructure engineers; incident responders; and authorized enterprise security teams.

### Authorized use and distribution
Training material is provided for defensive education. Users must comply with applicable laws, organization policies, platform submission rules, and evidence-handling requirements. Instructor solutions should be separated from student challenge packages to preserve assessment integrity.
