# Training & Educational Use Notice

EchoTrace — The Missing Evidence is a fictional cybersecurity training scenario developed exclusively for technical education, DFIR training, forensic engineering, incident response, system recovery, and business-continuity exercises.

All organizations, identities, systems, infrastructure, records, datasets, IP addresses, domains, events, failures, and attack activity are fictional or synthetically generated.

The exercise contains no executable malware, destructive payload, credential-stealing tool, real confidential information, or production infrastructure. Students work only with supplied evidence and authorized isolated laboratory resources.

The objective is defensive: preserve evidence, determine root cause, reconstruct outages, scope data loss, validate backups, recover systems safely, and communicate defensible conclusions.
