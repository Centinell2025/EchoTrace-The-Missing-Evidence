# LAB-11 — AI-Assisted Activity Assessment

**Role:** Senior forensic analyst

## Objective

Evaluate a claim that the incident involved AI-assisted targeted activity without treating the claim itself as evidence.

## Student tasks

- Establish the normal behavior of ORCH-01.
- Measure event cadence, concurrency, target selection, and adaptation visible in supplied telemetry.
- Compare suspicious activity against known legitimate automation.
- Identify evidence compatible with scripted automation.
- Identify evidence that may suggest adaptive automation.
- Identify what additional telemetry would be required to distinguish AI-assisted behavior from deterministic scripting.
- Assign a confidence level and explicitly document alternative explanations.

## Deliverable

AI / Automation Forensic Assessment containing observations, competing hypotheses, evidence supporting each hypothesis, evidence against each hypothesis, confidence, and additional collection requirements.

## Senior standard

The student receives full credit for concluding that AI involvement cannot be proven if that is what the evidence supports. The learning objective is forensic rigor, not confirmation of the scenario narrative.
