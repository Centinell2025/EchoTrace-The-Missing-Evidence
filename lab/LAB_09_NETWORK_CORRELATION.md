# LAB-09 — Network & Management-Plane Correlation

**Role:** Network forensic analyst

## Objective
Correlate ORCH-01 actions with independent network evidence.

## Tasks
- Map management flows to orchestration events.
- Separate approved baseline traffic from the unexplained JOB-X91 sequence.
- Determine the number and ordering of targeted systems.
- Identify what network telemetry proves and what it cannot prove.
- Build a UTC management-plane timeline.

## Deliverable
Network / Orchestration Correlation Matrix.

## Pass standard
Connections establish communication, not intent or human attribution.
