# LAB-03 — Identity & Administrative Context

**Role:** Identity forensic analyst

## Objective
Determine how identity dependencies influenced the outage and preserve attribution discipline.

## Tasks
- Map ID-01, ID-02 and AUTH-DB dependencies.
- Correlate authentication degradation with storage latency.
- Determine whether identity failure preceded or followed the first storage anomaly.
- Identify which evidence would be required to attribute administrative actions to a human operator.
- Recommend identity evidence to preserve before recovery.

## Deliverable
Identity Dependency Assessment.

## Pass standard
Service-account activity is not automatically attributed to a person.
