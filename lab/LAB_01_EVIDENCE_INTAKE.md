# LAB-01 — Evidence Intake & Chain of Custody

**Role:** Senior DFIR technician

## Objective
Establish a defensible evidence baseline before outage recovery begins.

## Tasks
1. Inventory every supplied evidence family.
2. Identify source system, acquisition time, time basis, and limitation.
3. Separate primary-host evidence from independently surviving telemetry.
4. Rank volatile and persistent evidence by preservation urgency.
5. Define the hashes and custody fields required when the dataset is frozen.
6. Identify evidence that recovery actions could overwrite.

## Deliverable
Evidence register and preservation order.

## Pass standard
The student explains why restoring service before preserving critical evidence may permanently reduce investigative confidence.
