# LAB-15 — Senior Incident Command Capstone

**Role:** DFIR lead / recovery incident commander

A critical enterprise environment is partially unavailable, sensitive datasets may be lost, and the backup platform cannot yet be trusted.

## Required deliverables

- evidence register and chain-of-custody record;
- normalized master timeline;
- dependency and failure-propagation diagram;
- identity and orchestration assessment;
- storage-forensics report;
- data-loss scope matrix;
- backup integrity and restore-point assessment;
- AI/automation assessment;
- preservation and containment plan;
- clean-room recovery sequence;
- validation tests for restored services;
- business-continuity priorities;
- executive incident statement.

## Decision standard

The recovery plan must not sacrifice forensic evidence merely to restore service quickly. The analyst must explain which systems can be restored, which require validation, and which evidence remains insufficient to declare data permanently lost.
