# LAB-06 — Data-Loss Scoping

**Role:** Data recovery forensic analyst

## Objective
Determine what is unavailable, damaged, recoverable, unverified, or potentially lost.

## Tasks
- Analyze data_scope.csv.
- Correlate primary-state observations with backup evidence.
- Do not label unavailable data permanently lost without recovery evidence.
- Identify the strongest and weakest recovery positions.
- Calculate the known-good recovery time for each dataset.
- Identify business data requiring additional acquisition.

## Deliverable
Data-Loss Scope Matrix.

## Pass standard
The student distinguishes availability loss from permanent data loss.
