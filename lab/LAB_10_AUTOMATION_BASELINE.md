# LAB-10 — Automation Baseline Analysis

**Role:** Forensic automation analyst

## Objective
Determine how JOB-X91 differs from approved orchestration.

## Tasks
- Compare target scope, timing, concurrency, identity, and change-ticket context.
- Calculate the event intervals for JOB-X91.
- Compare them with normal median gaps and parallel-action limits.
- Identify behavior outside the documented baseline.
- State whether the evidence proves compromise of ORCH-01.

## Deliverable
Automation Deviation Report.

## Pass standard
Deviation from baseline supports investigation but is not by itself proof of malicious control.
