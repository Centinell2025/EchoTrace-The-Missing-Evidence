# EchoTrace — 100 Digital Forensic Technology Scenarios

**Purpose:** advanced practical training in Digital Forensic Technology and Forensic Engineering.

Every scenario is fictional and uses synthetic evidence. The learner is expected to perform evidence-based analysis, preserve integrity, document uncertainty, and produce a professional forensic deliverable. No scenario requires malware execution or unauthorized access.

## Training model

Each scenario will contain five elements: **Incident Situation → Evidence Set → Practical Investigation → Required Deliverable → Instructor Solution**.

Student material may be published separately. Instructor solutions and answer keys must remain private until the repository is made private.

## Senior completion standard

A learner completing the full track should be able to acquire and preserve evidence, reconstruct complex timelines, analyze SQL/database artifacts, correlate identity/network/storage evidence, validate backups, distinguish unavailable data from permanent loss, evaluate automation/AI claims without overstatement, engineer a safe recovery sequence, and communicate technically defensible conclusions.

## Solution policy

Solutions will document the expected evidence path, commands/queries used for analysis where appropriate, expected findings, alternative defensible interpretations, confidence level, common analyst mistakes, and remediation/recovery reasoning. Solutions are instructor material and must not be exposed in a public challenge repository.
